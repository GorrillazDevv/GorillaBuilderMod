#Gorilla Builder Playground
A builder playground inside your own copy of Gorilla Tag. Everything you make stays on your PC and is never sent to other players.
Hold Y on your left controller to open the hand menu. Aim with your right hand and pull its trigger to pick something.
Five size mushrooms: Purple 0.2x, Blue 0.45x, Green normal, Red 2x and Gold 3x. Each has its own mushroom model and musical size-change sound.
Six animated tabs with 84 things: Size, Furniture, Decor, Build, Toys and Blasters. Cards show spinning previews, lift when you point at them, squash when pressed, and pages slide in.
Furniture: couch, chairs, beds, tables, desks, wardrobe, dresser, shelves, lamp, rug and more.
Decor: glowing TV, PC, arcade cabinet, vending machine, fridge, stove, bathtub, toilet, plants, a tree, a campfire, a street lamp and more.
Build pieces are anchored where you place them, so you can climb and stand on walls, platforms, ramps, stairs, pillars, arches, bridges, ladders, a tunnel, a goal, a watchtower and a trampoline that bounces toys.
Toys: ragdoll dummies (plain, zombie and robot), bouncy balls, basketball, soccer ball, floaty beach ball, a balloon that rises, bowling ball and pins, dominoes, dice, a rubber duck, a teddy bear, barrels, tires, an anvil, C4, a toy mine and a firework.
Blasters: Pop Blaster, Scatter Blaster, Rail Blaster, Bubble Blaster, Minigun, Gravity Gun (pulls toys toward you) and Boom Launcher (explodes where it hits). Press A to put a blaster away.
Placement only happens on a real floor or wall: the preview turns red when there is nowhere to place. Quick trigger presses are buffered so nothing is dropped. Everything you place sits on top of the surface and stays when you walk between areas; anything that slips out of the world comes back to where you placed it.
Hold right grip to move a placed object; tilt the stick to turn it. Right stick also turns the placement preview.
Place C4 and press B or Detonate to send nearby toys flying. Undo removes the last object. Press Clear twice to empty the playground.
Every menu action and toy has its own original synthesized sound effect.
Single player. Toys exist locally for this session and persist when the game joins a room. Limits are 64 placed objects, including up to ten dummies, ten charges and twenty build pieces. The desktop PC and screens are decorative props.
Requires the Windows Steam version of Gorilla Tag and VR controllers for normal play. Melty installs the bundled loader and starts Gorilla Tag. The host game's content is read from the player's own installed copy. This package includes no Gorilla Tag content or Nintendo game files. Mushroom meshes, props and sounds are original assets created for this mod.
